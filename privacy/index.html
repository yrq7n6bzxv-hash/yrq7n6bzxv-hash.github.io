Privacy Policy

Last updated September 3, 2026

The short version

The MacCopy app does not collect, transmit, sell, or share your clipboard data or personal information. Your clipboard history is stored locally on your Mac and is never sent to us.

Clipboard data

MacCopy reads the macOS system clipboard to provide clipboard history, search, pinning, organization, and paste features. Saved clipboard content is stored in the app’s local sandbox and encrypted at rest using cryptography provided by Apple’s operating system.

MacCopy does not operate a server and does not upload or synchronize your clipboard history. It has no user accounts, advertising, analytics, or tracking.

Preferences and permissions

Settings such as your history limits and app preferences are stored locally on your Mac. MacCopy may request Accessibility permission if you enable automatic pasting. This permission is used only to send the paste command to the active application; it is optional and can be revoked in macOS System Settings.

Retention and deletion

Clipboard items remain on your Mac according to the history limits you choose. You can remove individual items or clear history from within MacCopy. Uninstalling MacCopy does not necessarily remove its sandbox container; you can delete the app’s container from your user Library if you want to remove all remaining local data.

Support email

If you email us for support, we receive the information you choose to include, such as your email address and message. We use it only to respond to your request. Email is handled by the relevant email providers and is separate from the MacCopy app.

This website

This is a static website with no analytics, advertising, or tracking scripts added by us. It is hosted by GitHub Pages, so GitHub may process technical information needed to deliver the site under the GitHub General Privacy Statement.

Changes to this policy

If MacCopy’s privacy practices change, this page will be updated and the date above will be revised.
