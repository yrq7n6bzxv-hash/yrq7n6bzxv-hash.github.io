Privacy Policy

Arillora saves your garden progress on your device. The app does not send it to us.

Last updated September 5, 2026

Who provides Arillora?

Arillora is provided by Glauco Porzio. This policy covers the Arillora app, these support pages, and messages you choose to send for support.

Data in the app

The game works offline. It does not require an account or collect or transmit personal information or gameplay progress to the developer. It has no advertising, in-app purchases, tracking, or third-party analytics SDKs.

Completed garden identifiers and your best water-use result for each garden are stored locally in the app’s preferences (UserDefaults). They are used to remember completed gardens and unlock the next garden. An unfinished route is kept only during the current app session and is not saved for your next launch.

Network access and external links

Arillora does not make network requests to run the game or synchronize progress. If you choose a support or privacy link in the app, your system browser opens the relevant website. Visiting that website uses an internet connection and is subject to the website and hosting practices described below.

Local storage, backups, and deletion

Your saved progress stays in the app’s local storage until you remove it or the app’s data is removed. On iPhone and iPad, choosing Delete App removes the app and its local data; Offload App preserves that data.

Device backups may contain app data depending on your Apple and device settings. A restored backup may restore progress. Arillora does not provide cloud synchronization or operate a backup server. Backups and their deletion are controlled through Apple’s device and backup services.

Support email

If you contact support, Glauco Porzio receives the information you voluntarily send, including your email address, message, and any attachments. It is used to respond to and resolve your support request. Email is processed by the relevant email providers and is separate from the game.

For questions about a support message, or to request its deletion, email glaucoporzio@gmail.com. Please avoid including sensitive information that is not needed to describe your issue.

This website

These are static pages. We have added no analytics, advertising, tracking scripts, external fonts, or cookies. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical information, including IP addresses and request information, to deliver and protect the site under the GitHub General Privacy Statement.

Apple services

Apple’s App Store, operating system, device backups, and any device diagnostics you choose to share are governed by Apple’s settings and privacy practices. This policy describes Arillora’s own data practices.

Changes and contact

If Arillora’s data practices change, this policy and its update date will be revised. For privacy questions, contact Glauco Porzio at glaucoporzio@gmail.com.
