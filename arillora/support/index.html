How to play

  1. Start at the spring, marked by a water drop. Drag a continuous path, or tap each next square.
  2. Move up, down, left, or right. Each new square uses one water; the starting spring is free.
  3. Pass beside every flower. A flower is watered when it shares an edge with any square in your path; diagonals do not count.
  4. Finish at the pond within the water limit. You cannot pass through flowers or rocks, cross your own path, or continue beyond the pond.

Change your route

Use Undo to remove the last step, or tap an earlier square in your path to rewind to it. Both return the water used by the removed steps. Restart clears the current route so you can try again. Neither action removes your completed gardens.

Five gardens, at your own pace

Complete a garden to unlock the next. Open the garden picker using the four-square button near the title to revisit unlocked gardens. There is no timer, account, advertising, or in-app purchase.

A hint for the first garden

From the spring, move three squares to the right and finish at the pond. The stream passes beside both flowers.

What is saved?

Completed gardens and the least water you used for each are saved on this device. An unfinished path is not saved when you leave and relaunch the app. Arillora does not synchronize progress between devices.

Language and accessibility

Arillora is available in English, French, Spanish, and Italian. It follows your preferred supported app or device language. Where available, choose Arillora’s language in iOS Settings. You can tap squares instead of dragging, and game cells have VoiceOver labels. Text respects your device’s text-size settings.

Report a problem

Email your iOS version, your Arillora app version, the garden number, and the steps that caused the problem. A screenshot can help; remove personal information before sending it. Please do not send passwords or payment details.

Start over completely

To remove local progress on iPhone or iPad, use Delete App in iOS Settings. Offload App preserves app data. Restoring a device backup may restore saved progress. See the privacy policy for more about storage and backups.
