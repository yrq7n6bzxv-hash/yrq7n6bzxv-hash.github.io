Getting started

MacCopy runs on macOS 14 or later. Open the app and use Option–Command–C to show your clipboard history. Select an item to copy it back to the system clipboard.

Why does MacCopy request Accessibility access?

Accessibility permission enables automatic pasting into the active app. It is optional: MacCopy can still save and restore clipboard items without it. Manage this permission in System Settings → Privacy & Security → Accessibility.

Where is my history stored?

Your history is stored only in MacCopy’s sandbox on your Mac. Clipboard payloads are encrypted at rest, and MacCopy does not upload or synchronize them.

How do I remove clipboard items?

Delete individual entries or clear your clipboard history from inside MacCopy. History limits can also be adjusted in the app’s settings.

Reporting a problem

When contacting support, include your macOS version, what you expected to happen, and the steps that reproduce the issue. Do not include confidential clipboard content.
